About the founder

The person you talk to is the person who builds it.

Here you work with the people doing the building. I read every brief myself, I tell you when an idea will cost more than it is worth, and I stay on a project until it works the way you pictured it.

About us

A small studio that builds everything to order.

Nirmaan is a software studio. We design and build mobile apps, websites and business systems for companies that have outgrown off-the-shelf tools, and for ideas that do not exist yet.

We keep the team small on purpose, so the people you meet on the first call are the people who build your project.

  • Made from scratch

    No templates and no recycled themes. Your product is drawn and built around the way your team works.

  • One team, start to finish

    Design, development, testing and launch stay under one roof, so nothing gets lost between handovers.

  • Clear and direct

    Plain-language updates, agreed scopes and honest timelines from the very first conversation.
